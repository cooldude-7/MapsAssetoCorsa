Assetto Corsa Map Generator

dev build

Find it in Steam: right-click Assetto Corsa, then Manage, then Browse local files. Copy the path from the top of the window and paste it here.

Touge Battle: race one AI car from A to B on your tracks. It's a game mode for Custom Shaders Patch 0.2.11 or newer.

Offline roads

Roads come from OpenStreetMap's servers, which can take half a minute per area. Download Ontario's roads once and they load instantly.
