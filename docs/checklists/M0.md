# M0 in-game checklist: 12 test tracks

Thanks for testing! These 12 small tracks were made by our track generator. Each one checks
**one thing** about how Assetto Corsa (AC) reads our files. Your results tell us what works
before we build real roads.

You need: Assetto Corsa on Windows, Content Manager (CM), and about an hour.

Each test says **what it checks**, **what to do**, **what PASS looks like**, and **what to
write down if it fails**. At the end there is a reply template you can copy and fill in.

> For the developers: the tracks are built with
> `uv run drift-maps spike --out out/spike --zip out/drift-maps-m0-tests.zip`
> (code: `src/driftmaps/spike.py`). Each test maps to a spike in
> `docs/research/ac_format.md` §0.1 or `docs/research/ai_line.md`.

---

## Before you start

### 1. Install the tracks

1. Download `drift-maps-m0-tests.zip` (the file I sent you).
2. Find your AC tracks folder. Pick one way:
   - **Steam:** Library → right-click **Assetto Corsa** → **Manage** → **Browse local
     files**. A folder opens. Go into `content`, then `tracks`.
   - **Content Manager:** open **Content** → **Tracks**. Click any track. Click the
     **Folder** button at the bottom. A folder opens. Go **up one level**. You are now in
     `tracks`.
   - The usual place is `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\assettocorsa\content\tracks\`.
3. Right-click the zip → **Extract All...** → **Browse** → pick that `tracks` folder.
4. **Check the path.** Windows likes to add an extra folder named after the zip. You want
   this:

   ```
   ...\content\tracks\dm_t1_minimal\dm_t1_minimal.kn5     <- right
   ...\content\tracks\drift-maps-m0-tests\dm_t1_minimal\  <- wrong, move the dm_ folders up
   ```

   You should now have 12 new folders, `dm_t1_minimal` to `dm_t12_ai_ab`.
5. Close Content Manager and open it again.
6. Go to **Drive** → **Quick Drive**. Click the track picture. Type `DM T` in the search
   box. You should see 12 tracks named **DM T1 Minimal** to **DM T12 AI AB**.

If they don't show up, check the path in step 4 again.

### 2. Settings for every test

- **Car:** a normal road car from the base game, for example the **BMW M3 E30**. Use the
  same car for every test, and write down which one.
- **Mode:** each test says which one (Practice, Hotlap, Time Attack or Race). Pick it in
  the **Mode** list on the Quick Drive page. Then press **Go!**
- **Leaving a session:** press **Esc** → **Exit**.
- **If Content Manager says a tag is missing:** click the fix button next to the message,
  then press Go! again. It is harmless.
- **Screenshots:** press **F8** in game. They are saved in
  `Documents\Assetto Corsa\screens\`.

### 3. The log file (important when something fails)

AC writes what went wrong to:

```
Documents\Assetto Corsa\logs\log.txt
```

AC **overwrites** this file every time it starts. So when a test fails, copy `log.txt`
**right away**, before you start the next test. Rename the copy after the test, for example
`log_T3.txt`.

### 4. Do T1 first

**T1 is the most basic test. If T1 fails, stop there** and send me `log.txt` plus a screenshot.
None of the other tests can work if T1 does not.

Also tell me: **do you have Custom Shaders Patch (CSP)?** If you are not sure: in Content
Manager, open **Settings**. If there is a **Custom Shaders Patch** page with lots of options,
you have it. Write down the version if you can see it.

## T1: Minimal (DM T1 Minimal)

**What it checks:** can AC load the simplest possible track we make.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Track **DM T1 Minimal**. Go!
2. Wait for the car to appear. Look around (F1 changes the camera).
3. Drive slowly to the end of the straight (200 m) and back.

**PASS looks like**
- The track appears in CM and loads.
- The car sits on a grey road with grass on both sides.
- The car faces along the long straight.
- From the outside camera you can see the road from above (it is not invisible).

**If it fails, write down:** where it stopped (not in the list / crash to menu / stuck on
the loading screen / car falls through the ground / road invisible). Copy `log.txt`.
**Then stop and send it to me.**

## T2: Handedness (DM T2 Handedness)

**What it checks:** that left is really left, and the minimap is not mirrored.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Go!
2. Open the map: move the mouse to the right edge of the screen. A bar of apps appears.
   Click **Map**.
3. Drive slowly forward. Look at the blue sign far ahead, and at the blue arrow painted on
   the road.
4. Drive through the corner and to the end.

**PASS looks like**
- The painted arrow on the road says **LEFT** and points to your left.
- The road turns **left**.
- The tall blue sign straight ahead shows a normal **N** (not a mirrored И).
- The minimap shows the road going **up, then left**. Your dot stays on the road line.
- In Content Manager, the small track outline also goes up, then left.

**If it fails, write down:** which of these was wrong (road turned right? N mirrored?
minimap mirrored or upside down? dot off the road?). A screenshot of the minimap helps.

---

## T3: Invisible walls (DM T3 Walls)

**What it checks:** that invisible walls stop the car but are not drawn.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Go!
2. Drive **slowly** (walking pace, under 20 km/h).
3. Steer gently to the left, towards the small orange posts. Keep going until you hit
   something.
4. Do the same on the right side.
5. Drive to the end. The last 50 m has a red wall you can see. Touch that one too.

**PASS looks like**
- You bump into an invisible wall just before the orange posts, on both sides.
- You never see anything pink or purple (that would mean the "invisible" wall is drawn).
- The red wall at the end is also solid.

**If it fails, write down:** could you drive through the invisible walls? Did you see pink
or purple walls? Was the red wall solid?

## T4: A to B timing (DM T4 AB Timing)

**What it checks:** that a point-to-point timer starts, splits and stops.

The road has three pairs of coloured posts: **green** = start (50 m), **yellow** = split
(250 m), **red** = finish (450 m). After red there are 50 m of road, then a wall. **Brake
after the red posts.**

**Steps** (do this three times, once per mode)
1. Mode **Practice**. Go! Drive through green, yellow and red. Watch the timer (top left
   of the screen).
2. Exit. Mode **Hotlap**. Go! Same drive.
3. Exit. Mode **Time Attack**. Go! Same drive.

**PASS looks like**
- The timer starts when you pass the green posts.
- A split time shows at the yellow posts.
- The timer stops at the red posts and shows your time.

**Write down for each mode** (even if it passed): did the timer start, split and stop? What
happened after the red posts (nothing / session ended / car moved back to the start /
something else)?

## T5: Swapped gates (DM T5 Swapped Gates)

**What it checks:** that the timing posts only work when their left and right ends are the
right way round. Here we swapped them on purpose.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Go!
2. Drive through the green, yellow and red posts, like in T4.

**PASS looks like:** the timer does **not** start, split or stop. (Yes, here "nothing
happens" is the pass.)

**If it fails, write down:** the timer worked just like in T4.

---

## T6: Spawn heights (DM T6 Spawns)

**What it checks:** at which height the car should appear, and that it faces the right way.
There are three blue arrows painted on the road: **PIT 0.0 m**, **START 1.0 m** and
**HOTLAP 2.0 m**. The number is how high above the road we put the car.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Go! Watch the first two seconds closely. The car should appear next
   to the **PIT 0.0 m** arrow.
2. Exit. Mode **Time Attack**. Go! The car should appear next to **START 1.0 m**.
3. Exit. Mode **Hotlap**. Go! The car should appear next to **HOTLAP 2.0 m**.

**PASS looks like** (for each of the three)
- The car is next to the matching arrow, facing the way the arrow points.
- It does not fall, bounce hard, shake, or sink into the road.

**Write down for each one:** where the car appeared, which way it faced, and whether it
dropped, bounced or got stuck in the road. If one looked best, say which.

## T7: Textures (DM T7 Textures)

**What it checks:** which picture formats AC can show on the road.

The road has three lanes with the same grid pattern. Labels at the start say which lane is
which: **PNG** (left), **DDS** (middle), **NPOT** (right).

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Go!
2. Look at the three lanes and the labels.
3. Stay still and look far down the road (600 m). Then drive along it.

**PASS looks like**
- All three lanes show the grid pattern. None is black, white, or missing.

**Also write down:** which lanes shimmer, sparkle or flicker far away, and which look calm.
This is not a fail, just useful.

**If it fails, write down:** which lane looked wrong and how. A screenshot helps.

## T8: Track in several files (DM T8 Models Ini)

**What it checks:** that a track can be split into several files.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Go! You start on a short road piece marked **MAIN KN5**.
2. Drive forward. You should pass labels **CHUNK 1**, **CHUNK 2** and **CHUNK 3**.
3. Drive to the end (about 360 m).

**PASS looks like:** it loads, and the road and grass go on all the way to the end with no
missing pieces.

**If it fails, write down:** which pieces were missing (you would see a gap or fall
through). Copy `log.txt`.

## T9: Limits (DM T9 Limits)

**What it checks:** a very detailed road piece, some odd objects in the file, and objects
that should only appear when you are close.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Go!
2. Look around at the start. Then drive slowly to the end (about 320 m) and watch the posts.

**PASS looks like**
- It loads and you can drive the whole road.
- **Blue posts** stand along the left side.
- A **big red post** stands on the right, near the start.
- **Yellow posts** on the right only appear when they are within about 60 m. As you drive,
  new ones pop up ahead and old ones vanish behind you.

**Write down:** are the blue posts there? Is the red post there? Do the yellow posts pop
in and out, or are they always there, or never there? If it does not load, copy `log.txt`.

## T11: AI on a loop (DM T11 AI Loop)

**What it checks:** that AC accepts the AI driving line we generate, on a normal loop
track. It also gives the "ideal line" (the coloured driving line on the road).

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice**. Before pressing Go!, open **Assists** on the Quick Drive page and tick
   **Ideal line**. (In game, **Ctrl+I** also turns it on and off.) Go!
2. Drive one lap. Look at the ideal line.
3. Exit. Mode **Race**. Set **3 laps**, **3 opponents** (same car), AI strength about
   **90%**. Go!
4. Race the 3 laps, or just watch the AI for a while.

**PASS looks like**
- The ideal line is drawn along the road all the way round.
- In the race, the AI cars drive round the loop, lap times are counted, and the race ends
  after 3 laps with a results screen.

**If it fails, write down:** crash while loading (copy `log.txt`!), no ideal line, AI cars
not moving, AI cars driving off the road, or laps not counted. Also say if CSP was on.

**Optional:** if you have CSP, turn it off in Content Manager (Settings → Custom Shaders
Patch) and try step 1 once more. Then turn it back on. Write down what happened.

## T12: AI from A to B (DM T12 AI AB)

**What it checks:** the AI line on a point-to-point road. It is the T2 road, wider, with a
start (green posts) and a finish (red posts). After the finish there are 100 m of road,
then a wall.

**Steps**
1. Mode **Practice** with **Ideal line** ticked (see T11). Go! Drive to the end.
2. Exit. Mode **Race**, **1 lap**, **3 opponents**. Go!
3. Watch what the AI cars do at the red posts and after them.

**PASS looks like**
- It loads, and the ideal line follows the road from the start, through the corner, past
  the red posts.

**Write down** (this part is not pass or fail, we just need to know): what did the AI cars
do at and after the finish? For example: stopped, crashed into the wall, turned around,
stood still at the start. Did the race end and show results?
